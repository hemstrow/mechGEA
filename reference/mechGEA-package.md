# mechGEA: Mechanistic Bayesian Genotype-Environment Association Analyses with Likelihoods

A user-friendly interface for conducting Bayesian Genotype-Environment
Association (GEA) tests with genotype-likelihood or pool-seq data, based
on Goel et al. (2026)'s approaches incorporating migration rate
estimation in spatially structured populations.
